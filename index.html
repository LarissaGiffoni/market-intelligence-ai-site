About the Project

Market Intelligence AI is a software application designed to support product research, market analysis, pricing decisions and profitability assessment for e-commerce operations.

Planned Features

  • Supplier catalog management
  • Automated product research
  • Intelligent product matching
  • Market price comparison
  • Profitability and cost analysis
  • Market indicators and analytical reports
  • Sales opportunity identification
  • Machine learning and predictive analytics

Mercado Libre Integration

The application intends to integrate with authorized Mercado Libre API endpoints to retrieve permitted marketplace information and support internal business decisions.

The initial version will be used exclusively by its developer.

Data Security and Compliance

The project is designed to comply with applicable data protection regulations, API usage policies, authorization requirements and rate limits.

Commercial expansion and additional data processing will be subject to applicable permissions.

Development Status

The project is currently in its initial development and technical feasibility validation phase.

Planned technologies include Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Machine Learning and REST APIs.
